## About the four images that were added to the main folder

These weren't used, for these reasons:

- `Florida Virtual School Incorporates Face-to-Face___.jpg` is only 235 px wide, which is far too small for a website. It also appears to come from another school's article.
- `download (4).jpg` has the look of an AI-generated image (the laptop carries a made-up gold crest). It would undo the goal of a real, human-looking site.
- `download (5).jpg` shows another organisation's computer class, with branded T-shirts.
- The `June was mostly eventful…` image is from another organisation's event: a "Masterclass" banner and bioinformatics T-shirts are visible.

Using photos of other schools' students on Olive's website could mislead parents. Photos the school takes itself are always the best choice.
