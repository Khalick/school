Questions from parents

Can't find your question? Send us a message.

Who is Olive for?

Students preparing for Cambridge IGCSE (usually ages 14 to 16) and Cambridge International AS & A Level (usually ages 16 to 19), in families who homeschool or need more flexibility than a traditional timetable allows.

Is Olive a full-time school or a homeschool programme?

Olive is built for homeschooling families. Your child follows the Cambridge International curriculum with our teachers' support and a personal study plan, on a timetable that fits your family. Ask us how a typical week looks at your child's level.

Do you support KCSE private candidates?

Yes. We teach and prepare private candidates, help with KNEC registration and guide them to the exams. 2027 is the final KCSE series, so register in the next KNEC window. KCSE at Olive.

Can homeschooled students sit Cambridge exams in Kenya?

Yes. Homeschooled students usually sit Cambridge exams as private candidates at an approved exam centre, such as the British Council. Entry deadlines come well before the exams, so we help you choose a series and guide you through registration.

Are Cambridge qualifications recognised by universities?

Cambridge IGCSE and A Levels are accepted by universities around the world, including in Kenya, the UK, the US, Canada and Australia. Entry requirements differ by university and course, so it is worth checking the ones your child is aiming for. We are happy to help.

How much are the fees?

Our fees are kept affordable. Request the current fee guide and we will send it to you.

My child has been in the Kenyan curriculum. Can they move to Cambridge?

Many students move to Cambridge programmes from other curricula. The right starting point depends on your child's age, subjects and confidence, so we will talk it through and suggest a realistic plan.

Can we visit?

Yes. Visits are by appointment, so that we can give you proper time. Send us a message and we will arrange a day and share directions.

Still deciding?

Send us a message or call the school. A member of our team will answer your questions.
