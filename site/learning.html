Internationally recognised programmes

Cambridge IGCSE and AS & A Level are set by Cambridge International Education, part of the University of Cambridge. We also prepare students for Pearson Edexcel qualifications. Sitting the Kenyan national exam instead? See KCSE private candidates.

Cambridge IGCSE

Usually ages 14 to 16

The International General Certificate of Secondary Education is the world's most widely taken international qualification for this age group. Students study a broad mix of core and optional subjects, and build the habits every later stage depends on: clear writing, problem-solving and independent study.

Length
Usually two years
Grading
A* to G in most subjects
Exams
June or November series
Leads to
AS & A Level and other sixth-form routes

Cambridge AS & A Level

Usually ages 16 to 19

Students choose fewer subjects and study them in depth. The AS Level can be taken as a qualification on its own or as the first half of the full A Level, which gives families flexibility on the way to university.

Length
AS: one year. A Level: two years
Grading
A* to E at A Level, a to e at AS Level
Exams
June or November series
Leads to
University in Kenya and around the world

Pearson Edexcel

International GCSE and International AS & A Level

Pearson Edexcel qualifications come from Pearson, a UK awarding organisation, and are recognised by universities and employers around the world. Students follow the International GCSE and then the International AS & A Level, with the same teaching, study plans and exam guidance as our Cambridge students.

Grading
International GCSE: 9 to 1. International A Level: A* to E
Exams
Several sittings a year, including January and May to June
Structure
International A Levels are taken in units, which can be sat in different exam sessions
Leads to
University in Kenya and around the world

Homeschooling, with the structure of a school behind it.

Your child studies at home on a timetable that suits your family. We provide the teaching, the plan and the exam guidance, and we keep you informed all the way.

Homeschool support

What it includes

  • Experienced subject teachers who teach, set work and give feedback
  • A personal study plan with subjects, pace and a target exam series
  • Regular updates for parents on progress and next steps
  • Past papers and exam technique from the start
  • Guidance on exam entry, private-candidate registration and exam centres

Exam calendar

Cambridge holds its main exam series twice a year. Homeschooled students usually sit their exams as private candidates at an approved centre, and centres set their own entry deadlines, often months ahead. We help families choose a series early and register on time.

Cambridge publishes exact timetables each year. We will confirm the dates for your child's series.
