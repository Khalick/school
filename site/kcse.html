Who it is for

For learners sitting KCSE outside a regular school

Whether you are improving your grades or finishing what you started, Olive gives you the structure of a school while you study as a private candidate.

  • Repeating to improve your grades in some or all of your subjects
  • Returning to complete secondary school after time away
  • Homeschooled learners who want the Kenyan national qualification
  • Adults who need KCSE for work, further study or university entry

How to register

Four steps to the exam room

KNEC sets the rules and the registration window each year. We will confirm the current requirements with you.

  1. Talk to us

    Tell us your previous results, the subjects you need and when you want to sit the exam.

  2. Prepare your documents

    Usually your KCPE certificate (or an approved equivalent) and your ID. A birth certificate is accepted in some cases.

  3. Register with KNEC

    Within the official window, choosing at least seven subjects, including English, Kiswahili and Mathematics.

  4. Study and sit the exam

    Follow your study plan with Olive's teachers, then sit KCSE in October and November.

Questions

KCSE questions

Can't find your question? Send us a message.

Who can sit KCSE as a private candidate?

Learners who are not registered through a regular secondary school, including candidates repeating to improve their grades and those returning to finish secondary education. KNEC requires a KCPE certificate or an approved equivalent, and identification.

Can I repeat only some subjects?

KNEC allows former candidates to register as full or partial repeaters. We will look at your previous results with you and advise on the best combination.

How many subjects do I need?

At least seven, including English, Kiswahili and Mathematics, and up to nine. We help you choose subjects that suit your goals.

Is 2027 really the last KCSE?

Yes. The 2027 KCSE is the final series before the transition to Competency Based Education. If you need the qualification, register in the next KNEC window.

Can I study KCSE and Cambridge at Olive?

Olive offers both the Kenyan KCSE route for private candidates and the Cambridge IGCSE, AS & A Level route. Ask us which one fits your goals.
